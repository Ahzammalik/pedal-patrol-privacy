Pedal Patrol - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Pedal Patrol ("the game") is a road-safety bicycle game published by HMA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. The game has no accounts and no chat.

Your game progress (coins, unlocked bicycles, levels, stars and settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • the device advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from the IP address,
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and ad interactions.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options, or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

The game asks Google for general-audience ads only.

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Rewarded ads

Some ads are optional. If you choose to watch one, you receive in-game coins. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party licences

The game uses open assets: Microsoft Rocketbox characters (MIT licence), Poly Haven textures and skies (CC0) and the Lilita One font (SIL OFL).
